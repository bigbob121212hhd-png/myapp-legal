Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TestCreateDev ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App, including when you connect your TikTok account.

1. Who we are

TestCreateDev is a video editing application that lets you edit videos, publish them to your own TikTok account, and view statistics about your TikTok account and videos in a dashboard.

Contact: bigbob121212hhd@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

a) Information from your TikTok account (only after you authorize the App)

Depending on the permissions (scopes) you grant, we may receive:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats): follower count, likes count, and video count.
  • Video information (video.list): a list of your published videos and their metadata, such as title, cover image, and view, like, comment, and share counts.
  • Publishing permission (video.publish / video.upload): the ability to send a video you created to your TikTok account only when you explicitly choose to publish it.
  • Access tokens: authorization tokens issued by TikTok that allow the App to act on your behalf within the granted permissions.

We never receive or store your TikTok password.

b) Content you create in the App

Videos and project files you edit in the App. Editing is performed locally on your device and files are not uploaded to our servers, except when you publish to TikTok.

c) Technical information

We do not collect analytics or tracking data.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate you and connect your TikTok account.
  • To publish videos to your TikTok account when you request it.
  • To display your TikTok statistics in your dashboard.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

We do not use TikTok data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the features described above.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share TikTok data with third parties, except where required by law. We do not currently use third-party hosting or service providers to process your data.

5. Data storage, security, and retention

  • Access tokens are stored in encrypted form and used only to perform actions you request.
  • TikTok statistics are stored in our database and refreshed periodically to keep your dashboard up to date.
  • We keep your data only as long as your account is connected or as needed to provide the App. When you disconnect your TikTok account or request deletion, we delete your tokens and TikTok-related data within 30 days.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke access: you can disconnect TikTok at any time in the App (Settings > Accounts > Disconnect TikTok) or in your TikTok account settings under apps and permissions.
  • Delete your data: email us at bigbob121212hhd@gmail.com and we will delete the data associated with you.
  • Access / correction: you may request a copy of the data we hold about you or ask us to correct it.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you also have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Third-party services

Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for TikTok's practices.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by other appropriate means.
